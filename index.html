Fefe, Nifemi, Her Royal Majesty (self-proclaimed)

I call you my DOM Queen, my beautiful angel, babygirl, and more to come.

You are a sweet soul, troublesome most times, but a sweet girl. You have a wonderful personality, great energy, and great vibes. Your anger can be triggered with a snap of a finger, and let's not talk about getting overstimulated. What I am trying to say is that you are a beautiful lady with a wonderful heart, kind (on occasions), special, unique, weird (the good kind), and likeable (debatable).

On your birthday, I hope this new chapter brings you genuine happiness, peace, good health, and everything you've been praying for. You're a beautiful person, inside and out, and you deserve so many good things. May this year give you more reasons to smile, beautiful memories to keep, and people who truly appreciate you. Keep being the amazing person you are. Happy birthday, Nifemi.
